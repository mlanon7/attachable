Common questions

Where do I find the max size?
On the upload page, usually next to the Upload or Browse button, like "PDF or JPG, max 5 MB". If it isn't there, the site's help page usually says. Then tap File & size in Attachable and choose or type it.

Does the max size apply to everything together?
For a Combined PDF it applies to the file (or to each part, if you allowed several files). With Separate PDFs or JPG pictures it applies to each file. Check the website's wording.

It says "Can't fit and stay readable".
Use the fixes it offers (make text pages Gray, turn PDF pages into pictures), crop margins away, remove pages you don't need, or choose a bigger size if the site allows it.

Where did my file go?
Into the folder you chose in Save to Files: Attachable shows its name. In Files, look in that folder; it may not appear under Recents right away. On the website, tap Upload or Browse, choose Files, and go to that folder.

The camera won't open.
Turn on Camera for Attachable in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera, or add pages from Photos or Files.

Does it need an account or the internet?
No. Everything Attachable does with your pages happens on your iPhone. Photos kept only in iCloud need a connection to download when you add them.
