Common questions

Where do I find the max size?
On the upload page, usually next to the Upload or Browse button, like "PDF or JPG, max 5 MB". If it isn't there, the site's help page usually says. Then, in Attachable, tap Max size at the top of your PDF (or in New PDF) and choose or type it.

Why did my max size change?
The slider's smallest size is an estimate for these pages. A size below it moves up to it, and yours is remembered: it moves back toward yours when the pages get smaller (Grayscale, fewer pages). The website's limit stays the same: after Make, check the real size.

Does the max size apply to everything together?
With one document it applies to the PDF (or to each part, if you allowed several files). With several documents or JPG pictures it applies to each file. Check the website's wording.

It says "Can't fit … at the lowest quality setting".
Use the fixes it offers (make text pages Gray, turn PDF pages into pictures), crop margins away, remove pages you don't need, or choose a bigger size if the site allows it.

Can I change text or a signature after adding it?
Yes. Text, signatures and drawings stay changeable: tap one on the page to move it, change its words, ink color, font or size, or remove it, even after you reopen the PDF. Put on one page makes them part of the new picture.

What's the difference between Saved papers and Save to Files?
Saved papers (at the top of your PDF list) keeps papers you upload again and again, like an ID or a pay stub, inside Attachable, ready to add to any PDF. Save them there from Photos, the Camera or Files, or with Select and Keep in a PDF. Save to Files puts a finished file in a folder for uploading.

Where did my file go?
Into the folder you chose in Save to Files: Attachable shows its name. In Files, look in that folder; it may not appear under Recents right away. On the website, tap Upload or Browse, choose Files, and go to that folder.

The camera won't open.
Tap Open Settings on the camera screen and turn on Camera (or Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → Attachable), or add pages from Photos or Files.

How do I lock Attachable?
Tap the padlock at the top of your PDF list to open Privacy & lock. There you can lock Attachable with Face ID (or Touch ID or your passcode), hide it in the app switcher, and delete made PDFs after a day, a week or 30 days. The padlock is shut while the lock is on.

Does it need an account or the internet?
No account. Attachable works on your pages on your iPhone. Photos and files kept only in iCloud or another cloud folder need a connection to download when you add them.
