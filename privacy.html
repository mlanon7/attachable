Privacy Policy

Attachable for iPhone · Effective November 9, 2026

In short: Attachable works on your documents on your iPhone. It never uploads them to us or to any AI service, and it has no account, ads, analytics or tracking. A document leaves your iPhone only if you send or save it somewhere yourself, or through your iPhone's backups. If you email us, we receive what you send.

Who we are

Attachable is an iPhone app by Martin Lashgari that turns photos and files into a PDF (or JPG pictures) under a website's upload size limit. Questions about this policy: attachable@projectcostpro.com.

Your documents are kept on your iPhone

  • The pages you add (photos, camera pictures, pictures you paste, PDFs and files), the files Attachable makes, the documents you keep in My documents, your signature and initials, the texts you type and your settings are kept in Attachable's own storage on your iPhone. Attachable processes them there. A locked PDF you unlock is kept without its password.
  • Attachable never uploads them to us, and it doesn't use any AI service. We have no servers that receive them.
  • Attachable contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code, and has no account.
  • If you choose in your iPhone's Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us crash reports and usage statistics, under Apple's privacy policy. They don't include your documents.

When something goes online

Attachable doesn't go online to work on your documents. Your iPhone may go online when you ask for something that lives elsewhere:

  • adding a photo that is kept only in iCloud, or a file from a cloud folder in Files (iCloud Drive, Google Drive and so on): iOS or that provider downloads it;
  • saving a made file to a cloud folder, or sharing, emailing or uploading it: it goes where you send it, through iOS and the app or service you choose, under their privacy policies;
  • opening this policy from Help: Safari loads this page (hosted on GitHub Pages, under GitHub's privacy statement).

Backups

Like other apps' files, Attachable's files can be included in your iPhone's backups (iCloud or a computer), under Apple's terms. Attachable doesn't currently exclude them.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write or attach, including any details you paste from Help → Copy details for a report (the app and iOS versions). Mail to our address is forwarded by our email forwarding service (ImprovMX) to our email inbox (Gmail, by Google), which handle it under their own privacy policies, and may process it outside your country, including in the United States. We use your messages only to answer you, and don't sell them or give them to anyone else for their own use. We keep them only as long as we need them to help you. You can ask for a copy of your messages, or for them to be deleted, by emailing the same address. If you're in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority. Please don't send your documents: describe the problem, or black out anything personal first.

Permissions

  • Photos: Attachable uses Apple's photo picker, so it sees only the photos you pick.
  • Camera: to photograph pages. The pictures stay in Attachable as pages of your PDF and go only into the file you make.
  • Add to Photos: only when you choose Save Image for a picture Attachable made.
  • Paste: Attachable reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste (iOS asks you first).
  • Face ID: only if you turn on the privacy lock. iOS checks your face; Attachable never sees or stores it.

You can change these at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

Keeping and deleting

You can delete any PDF or kept document in the app. The Privacy setting can lock the app with Face ID, hide it in the app switcher, and delete each made PDF a day, a week or 30 days after it was last made or changed (the next time Attachable's list of PDFs is open after that); PDFs not made yet, and My documents, are kept until you delete them. A page you remove or replace stays on your iPhone until Attachable next opens, so Undo can bring it back. A camera photo not yet added to its PDF is kept until it is added, you discard it, or you delete that PDF; while one is waiting, that PDF isn't deleted automatically. If Attachable ever finds its saved list damaged, it keeps that list and its files for up to 30 days so nothing is lost by mistake, then deletes them. Deleting the app removes everything it keeps on your iPhone; copies you saved, shared or emailed, and older backups, are not affected.

Children

Attachable isn't directed to children under 13, and collects nothing automatically from anyone. Anything a child emails us is handled as above, and deleted on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
