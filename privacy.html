Privacy Policy

Attachable for iPhone · Effective November 9, 2026

In short: Attachable collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing anywhere. Your pages stay on your iPhone unless you share, email, save or upload a file yourself.

Who we are

Attachable is an iPhone app by Martin Lashgari that turns photos and files into a PDF (or JPG pictures) under a website's upload size limit. Questions about this policy: attachable@projectcostpro.com.

What Attachable does with your information

  • Your pages (photos, camera pictures, PDFs and files you add), the files it makes, your signature and initials, the texts you type and your settings are kept in Attachable's own storage on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No data collection. We don't collect, store or receive any personal data, usage data, diagnostics or identifiers. Attachable contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code, and no AI service.
  • No network use of its own. Attachable works offline. The only time your iPhone goes online for it is when iOS downloads a photo you chose that is kept only in iCloud.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.

Permissions

  • Photos: to add the photos you choose. Attachable sees only the ones you pick.
  • Camera: to photograph pages. Pictures go only into your PDF.
  • Add to Photos: only when you choose Save Image for a picture Attachable made.
  • Face ID: only if you turn on the privacy lock. iOS checks your face; Attachable never sees or stores it.

You can change these at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

When a file leaves your iPhone

Only when you choose to: Share, Email, Save to Files (for example to iCloud Drive) or uploading the file to a website. Those go through iOS and the app or service you choose, under their own privacy policies.

Backups

Like other apps' files, Attachable's files can be included in your iPhone's backups (iCloud or a computer), under Apple's terms.

Keeping and deleting

You can delete any PDF or kept document in the app. The Privacy setting can delete made PDFs after a day, a week or 30 days, lock the app with Face ID and hide it in the app switcher. Deleting the app removes everything it keeps on your iPhone; copies you saved, shared or emailed, and older backups, are not affected.

Children

Attachable doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
