Attachable

Photos to PDF. Set the limit.

Attachable is an iPhone app for uploads that ask for "PDF, max 2 MB". Add photos, camera pages and files, put them in order, choose what the website wants (one PDF, a PDF per document, or JPG pictures) and its size limit. Attachable aims under the limit and shows the real size once the file is made. If the pages can't fit, even at the lowest quality setting, it says so and offers ways to fit, like making text pages gray, turning PDF pages into pictures or removing pages.

  • Works on your documents on your iPhone. No account.
  • Never uploads your documents to us or to any AI service. No analytics or tracking.
